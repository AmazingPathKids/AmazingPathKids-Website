About us

Calm tools for the early years. Starting with UpPrism.

Amazing Path Kids is a company in Ontario, Canada. We make UpPrism, an app that helps parents and caregivers of children aged 1 to 5 make sense of what they notice and decide what to do next.

Why we exist

Parents notice a lot. Most help stops at the noticing.

A new word, a skipped step, a habit that seems different from other children’s. Most of the help available sits at two extremes: long checklists that can frighten, or reassurance with nothing to act on.

We started Amazing Path Kids to fill the space between. Clear information about what you described, a sensible place to start, and something useful to do at home today.

What we believe

Four ideas behind every screen.

  1. 01

    Clarity over jargon

    If a parent has to look up a word, we have written it wrong. Every explanation in UpPrism is in plain language.

  2. 02

    One useful next step

    A tired parent needs one thing to try today, with the reason it helps. Not a list of twenty.

  3. 03

    Support, not diagnosis

    UpPrism helps you notice and act. Questions about a diagnosis belong with a qualified professional, and we say so.

  4. 04

    Privacy as a feature

    Information about your child is not our product. We collect what the app needs and do not sell personal information.

The product

UpPrism, our first app.

UpPrism turns a short check-in into a plain-language profile, three suggested priorities you can change, and home activities with steps and a reason. Over time it keeps a history of what you tried.

It is in development and not yet available in app stores. Scores and priorities come from transparent rules, and AI only helps with the wording.
